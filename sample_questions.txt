[SECTION] Quantitative Aptitude

Q: What is the ratio of 12 to 16?
A. 3:4
B. 4:3
C. 2:3
D. 3:2
ANSWER: A

Q: If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5, then A:B:C is?
A. 8:12:15
B. 2:3:5
C. 4:6:5
D. 8:12:10
ANSWER: A

Q: Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If each number is increased by 10, the ratio becomes 5:7. The numbers are?
A. 15, 25
B. 12, 20
C. 9, 15
D. 6, 10
ANSWER: A

Q: The ratio of incomes of two persons is 9:7 and the ratio of their expenditures is 4:3. If each of them saves Rs 2000, find the income of A?
A. Rs 18000
B. Rs 14000
C. Rs 16000
D. Rs 20000
ANSWER: A

Q: A and B together have Rs 1210. If 4/15 of A's amount is equal to 2/5 of B's amount, how much does B have?
A. Rs 484
B. Rs 460
C. Rs 550
D. Rs 726
ANSWER: A

[SECTION] Reasoning

Q: In a certain code language, 'MOUSE' is written as 'PRXVH'. How is 'HOUSE' written in that code?
A. KRXVH
B. IRXVH
C. LRXVH
D. JRXVH
ANSWER: A

Q: Which number comes next in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 42
B. 40
C. 38
D. 36
ANSWER: A

Q: If 'PAPER' is coded as 'SDSHU', then 'PENCIL' is coded as?
A. SHQFLO
B. SHQFOL
C. SHQFLN
D. SGQFLO
ANSWER: A

Q: Find the odd one out: 3, 5, 11, 14, 17, 21
A. 14
B. 21
C. 11
D. 17
ANSWER: A

Q: Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother or sister but that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?
A. His son
B. His father
C. His nephew
D. His own
ANSWER: A

A. Malevolent
B. Kind

A. since
B. for

D. by

A. have → has
B. their → his
C. Each → Every
D. Both A and B

A. Accommodate
B. Accomodate
C. Acommodate
